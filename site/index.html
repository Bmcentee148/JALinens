How It Works

One Call, Then You Stop Thinking About It

No sales process, no six-week onboarding. Most new accounts are running within the week.

You Call Us

Tell us what you go through in a week. We'll talk through sizes, counts and how often you need a stop. Straight pricing, no contract to sign.

We Set Your Par

We figure out the right quantity so you're never short and never paying for linen sitting in a closet. Adjust it any time as business changes.

We Run the Route

Same driver, same day each week. Clean goods in and counted onto your shelf, soiled goods out. If something's off, you call and we fix it.
